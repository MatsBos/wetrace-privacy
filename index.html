Wetrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Wetrace (“the app”) lets you keep traces — photos with a short description and an optional location — on shared timelines with friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have.

The app is provided by Bos.Dev (“we”, “us”). You can contact us at bos.devsupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information When it is collected Why
Email address, password (stored hashed), username and optional full name When you create an account or sign in with email To create and secure your account and let friends find you
Google account identifier, name, email and profile picture If you choose “Sign in with Google” To sign you in and pre-fill your profile
Profile picture If you upload one To show it to you and your friends
Photos, descriptions and notes you post When you add a trace or a note To provide the core feature: shared timelines
Location (latitude, longitude and place name) Only if you allow location access and attach a location to a trace To show where a trace was made and to display it on the map
Friends and timeline membership When you send or accept friend requests or join timelines To decide who can see what
Push notification token and device platform If you allow notifications To notify you when someone adds a trace to a timeline you are on
Advertising identifier and device/usage data used for ads When the app shows ads (Android and iOS) To show and measure advertising — see “Advertising” below
Theme preference When you change it in Settings Stored only on your device

We do not use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services in the app. Our servers keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, time and request type) for security and to keep the service running.

Camera, photos and location permissions

The app asks for permission to use your camera and photo library to add pictures, and for your location to attach a place to a trace. Each permission is optional: you can deny it and still use the rest of the app, and you can change it at any time in your device settings. Location is read when you add a trace; the app does not track your location in the background.

Who can see your information

  • Members of your timelines can see the traces, locations, descriptions and notes on those timelines, and the usernames and profile pictures of other members.
  • Friends can see your username and profile picture.
  • Picture files are served from public web addresses that are hard to guess, but anyone who has the exact link can open the picture. Do not post anything you would not be comfortable being reachable by link.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use the following providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf or under their own terms:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications (receives your push token and notification content). Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google AdMob — shows advertising (see below).
  • Map and place services — the map screen loads map tiles from a map tile server, which receives your IP address and the area you view. Place names are looked up from coordinates by your device’s geocoding service.
  • Our hosting provider My own server in belgium — stores the account data, photos and notes described above.

Advertising

On Android and iOS the app shows ads, supplied by Google AdMob, inside your timeline. AdMob may collect and use your device’s advertising identifier, IP address and information about your device and app use to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google’s consent message. You can withdraw or change your choice in the app where offered, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). For more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

How long we keep your information

We keep your account and the content you post until you delete it or ask us to delete your account. A trace you delete is removed from our servers together with its picture. Routine server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a trace: the person who posted a trace can delete it from the trace screen. This removes the picture permanently.
  • Delete your account and all associated data: email us at bos.devsupport@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my Wetrace account”. We will delete your account, profile, traces, notes, friendships and push tokens within 30 days and confirm by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, contact us at the address above. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep it secure (legitimate interests), and, for personalized ads and optional permissions, with your consent.

Security

Data is sent between the app and our servers over encrypted connections, and passwords are stored hashed. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Wetrace is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our servers and the providers listed above may be located outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed; for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
